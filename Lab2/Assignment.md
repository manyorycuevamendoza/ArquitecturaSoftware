## Case study

**Machinery Leasing — High difficulty — Suggested duration: 4 hours**

Corporate and SME lending is growing. Companies commonly work on projects and need machinery to execute them. Because payment for a project is often received only when the project finishes, these companies cannot finance all the required equipment and machinery upfront.

You have been hired as an architect to design the architecture of a machinery-leasing company in Peru.
